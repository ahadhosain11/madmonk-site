Delete Your Mad Monk Account

If you'd like to permanently delete your Mad Monk account and all associated data, follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

Send an email to support@madmonk.site from the email address associated with your Mad Monk account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."

Please include:

  • The email address you used to sign in to Mad Monk (via Google)
  • A short confirmation that you want your account permanently deleted

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in information
  • Your profile photo, if you uploaded one
  • Your cloud-backed-up focus session history (Premium users)
  • Your subscription will also be cancelled if active — you may also cancel it directly anytime via Google Play's subscription settings

Processing time

We will process your deletion request within 30 days and confirm by email once complete.

Note: Free-tier users' local data (focus history stored only on-device) is not covered by this process — that data can be removed at any time simply by uninstalling the app.
