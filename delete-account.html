Delete Your Mad Monk Account

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Mad Monk is provided by Md Ahad Hosain, an individual developer publishing on Google Play as Bengal Tiger Studio. You can delete your account in the app or request deletion by email without reinstalling it.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to the Mad Monk account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile → Delete account, below Log Out.
  3. Read the account, purchase, and deletion details and confirm the acknowledgement.
  4. Confirm deletion. For a verified renewing plan, the action also asks to cancel renewal. Wait for the app to confirm completion.

If the app reports a failure, deletion has not been confirmed. Follow its retry/support instructions and check your Google Play subscription status.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Email support@madmonk.site with the subject Account Deletion Request, preferably from the Google email used for Mad Monk. Include:

  • The Google sign-in email associated with your Mad Monk account.
  • Confirmation that you want your account and associated app data permanently deleted.
  • Whether you have an active Mad Monk subscription and want renewal cancelled as part of the request.

We verify ownership and cancellation authorization before acting. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, or payment-card details.

Processing time

We will process your email deletion request within 30 days and confirm by email once complete. If more information is needed to verify ownership, we will contact you. The in-app flow processes the request directly when its checks succeed.

What gets deleted

  • Your Mad Monk authentication account and stored email.
  • Your profile and uploaded photo.
  • Your tasks, focus history, progress, and associated active cloud records.
  • For in-app deletion, the account's saved app data on the device completing the flow, including for free users.

Your Google/Gmail account and Google's purchase records are separate and are not deleted.

Subscriptions and refunds

The in-app flow asks permission to stop renewal of verified Mad Monk subscriptions associated with your account and checks cancellation before completing deletion. Monthly/yearly renewing plans can have future renewals stopped; lifetime purchases have no recurring renewal.

Sending an email, logging out, or uninstalling alone does not stop billing. Check or cancel renewal directly in Google Play subscriptions using the purchasing account. Tell support about purchases not shown in the deletion flow.

Cancellation does not automatically refund a purchase. Refund eligibility follows Google Play's policies and applicable consumer rights.

If you return later

Sign in using the original Google identity and use the Google Play account that purchased Premium. Restore purchases can recover remaining paid subscription access or a valid lifetime purchase after verification. Expired, refunded, or revoked purchases do not grant access. Deleted tasks, history, photos, and progress cannot be recovered.

Limited records retained

For eligible restoration and fraud prevention, we retain coded purchase and Google identity/account fingerprints, product type, and paid expiry. These pseudonymous records do not contain your deleted email, name, photo, tasks, or history. Subscription recovery records are removed after expiry during daily cleanup. Lifetime recovery records remain for lifetime restoration.

Contact support if you also want recovery records removed; this prevents automatic recovery through that mechanism. A temporary deletion-receipt fingerprint may remain for up to 24 hours plus the daily cleanup interval. Incomplete deletion jobs may keep an account reference until resolved.

Support emails, provider logs, and technical backups have separate retention as explained in our Privacy Policy. Deleting active app records does not promise immediate removal from every log or backup. Completed deletion requests must be reapplied before a restored backup serves users.

Other devices and local copies

An email request cannot remotely erase offline data on your phone or other devices. Clear Mad Monk's app storage or uninstall it on those devices to remove local copies. Android/Google-managed device backups are separate; use your device or Google backup settings to manage them.
