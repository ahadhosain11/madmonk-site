Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Mad Monk ("we," "our," or "the app") is a focus and productivity app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account, used solely to create and identify your Mad Monk account.

Focus session data. We record the focus sessions you complete — duration, mode selected, and completion time — to power your history, streaks, and level progression within the app.

Task data. Any to-do items you create are stored so you can view and manage them within the app.

Profile photo. If you choose to upload a custom profile photo, it is stored securely and used only to display your avatar within the app.

Purchase information. If you subscribe to Mad Monk Premium, purchase transactions are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — only confirmation that a purchase was made, to unlock Premium features on your account.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the core features of the app (focus sessions, tasks, history, levels)
  • To back up your data to the cloud if you are a Premium subscriber
  • To personalize your experience (level titles, unlocked characters, streaks)
  • To improve the app based on aggregate, non-identifying usage patterns

3. Data Storage

Free users: your focus session history and task data are stored locally on your device only. This data is automatically cleared at the start of each new month, and will be permanently lost if you uninstall the app.

Premium subscribers: your focus session history is additionally backed up securely to our cloud database (hosted by Supabase), so it survives app reinstalls and is available across devices signed into the same account.

4. Third-Party Services

Mad Monk uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Google Sign-In — for account authentication
  • Google Play Billing — for processing Premium subscription payments
  • Supabase — for secure cloud database and file storage
  • Google AdMob — free-tier users may see advertisements served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers for ad personalization in accordance with Google's own privacy policy

5. Notifications and Permissions

Mad Monk may request the following device permissions, each used only for their stated purpose:

  • Notification access — to send you focus reminders and session alerts
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional, user-enabled) — to silence other notifications during your focus sessions, only if you turn this on in Settings

6. Data Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Free-tier local data can also be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Mad Monk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
