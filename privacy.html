Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how Mad Monk (“the App”) handles information when you use Mad Monk: Focus & Study Timer and the website at madmonk.site.

1. Who operates Mad Monk

Mad Monk is operated by Md Ahad Hosain under the developer name Bengal Tiger Studio. For privacy questions, contact support@madmonk.site.

2. Information the App uses

  • Google sign-in and account details: when you sign in, Google and Supabase provide the authentication and account information needed to identify your account, such as your email address, display name, and account identifier.
  • App profile and progress: profile settings and progress such as focus time, level, streak, and character selections.
  • Tasks and focus history: task details and completed focus sessions that you create in the App.
  • Premium status: subscription or purchase information needed to verify Premium access and its entitlement period. Google Play processes payment details; Mad Monk does not receive your full payment card number.

Free users’ task and focus-history data is primarily kept on the device. Some account and Premium-entitlement information is processed online to support sign-in and service operation. When Premium cloud sync is available and active for your account, supported profile, task, and focus data is synchronized with Supabase. Cloud sync availability depends on your current entitlement and the App’s sync settings.

Data kept only on your device can be lost if you uninstall the App, clear its storage, or lose the device. Cloud sync is not a substitute for a separate export or backup.

3. Advertising

The free version may display banner advertisements served through Google AdMob. Active Premium accounts do not see in-app ads while Premium access is verified. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address (which can indicate general location), ad and app interactions, diagnostics, and device or advertising identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. The data handled can depend on the SDK version, device settings, and your choices.

Google may use consent and privacy settings applicable to your region. You can review Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

4. How information is used

We use information to provide Google sign-in, keep your profile and app progress, synchronize supported data for Premium, verify purchases, provide support, protect the App from abuse, and operate and improve the service. Ad-related information is handled by Google for advertising and related measurement as described above.

5. Service providers and sharing

Mad Monk uses Google for sign-in, Google Play for app distribution and billing, Supabase for authentication and cloud database services, and Google AdMob to serve ads in the free version. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy documentation. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users, the App, or our rights.

6. Storage and retention

Information remains on your device or with the relevant service provider for as long as needed to provide the feature, maintain your account, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, or protect the service. Provider logs or backup copies may persist for a limited period after a change or deletion request under the provider’s normal retention processes.

7. Your choices and account deletion

  • You can stop using Google sign-in by logging out in the App.
  • You can manage or cancel a Google Play subscription in Google Play using the Google account that made the purchase.
  • You can request account deletion in the App from Profile → Delete account. The deletion flow removes the account and associated app data from active systems and attempts to cancel a linked subscription. Check Google Play subscriptions afterward to confirm the subscription’s billing status.
  • If you cannot access the App, contact support@madmonk.site for help with a deletion request.

Deleting the account does not erase information already held independently by Google Play or Google under their own policies, and information may remain temporarily in provider backups or records required by law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections for online requests. No online service or device storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children

Mad Monk is a general productivity app and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The revised date will appear at the top of this page. Please review this page periodically.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy: support@madmonk.site.

Note: The Play Store Data safety form must also describe the data practices of the App and its SDKs. This web policy does not update that form automatically.
